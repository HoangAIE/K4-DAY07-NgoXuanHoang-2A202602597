# Customer Support Playbook

## 1. Overview
This playbook provides standard operating procedures for customer service representatives.
Our goal is to resolve customer inquiries quickly, politely, and accurately.

## 2. Response SLA
- High Priority (System Outage, Billing Issue): First response within 15 minutes.
- Medium Priority (Account Settings, General Inquiry): First response within 2 hours.
- Low Priority (Feature Requests, Feedback): First response within 24 hours.

## 3. Communication Standards
- Always greet the customer by name.
- Empathize with customer issues before proposing solutions.
- Confirm resolution before closing tickets.
- Document recurring problems in the knowledge base.
